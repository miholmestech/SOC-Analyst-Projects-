# Security Awareness Brief

---

## Phishing Alert

A phishing email was recently identified attempting to impersonate a legitimate sender. 

The message encouraged recipients to reply regarding a financial opportunity and did not contain proper email authentication protections. 

Messages with these characteristics are commonly associated with phishing attempts designed to trick recipients into responding or sharing sensitive information.

## What Employees Should Do

If you receive an unexpected message that asks for financial information or requests a reply:

- Do not respond to the message.
- Do not share sensitive information.
- Report the email to your security or IT team.


Employees should avoid responding to unexpected messages and report suspicious emails to the security team.
